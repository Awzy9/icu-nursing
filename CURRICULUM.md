## First release: 40 P1 topics

Selection logic: highest bedside frequency and risk for new/transitioning ICU nurses, at least one P1 topic per domain except Domain 10 (emergencies are served by the simulation engine and cross-reference P1 topics; they are scheduled right after P1), and prerequisites before advanced topics.

**D1** Oxygen delivery & therapy · Invasive MV & modes · Ventilator parameters · Ventilator alarms · Suctioning · ETT care & cuff pressure · Weaning/SBT/extubation prep · Recognising respiratory deterioration
**D2** ECG & rhythm recognition · Arterial BP & art line · Shock recognition · Septic shock care · Vasopressor/inotrope monitoring · Cardiac arrest & post-arrest care · Defibrillation safety
**D3** Neuro assessment & GCS · ICP/CPP · Sedation monitoring (RASS) · Delirium screening
**D4** AKI · Urine output & fluid balance · Potassium disorders · ABG interpretation · CRRT fundamentals
**D5** Sedatives · Vasopressors · High-alert medication & infusion pump safety
**D6** Sepsis recognition · Standard/transmission precautions · CLABSI · VAE prevention
**D7** Head-to-toe assessment · Pain assessment · Pressure injury · Enteral feeding & aspiration
**D8** Coagulation tests · Transfusion & reactions
**D9** SBAR/handover/escalation · Documentation · Scope of practice

Release order within P1: 1) cross-cutting safety (9, 5-safety), 2) assessment foundations, 3) organ-system topics.

## Competency framework

An **original** framework (Novice → Transitioning → Experienced) will be written for the platform. AACN documents are benchmarks only and are not copied (see `LICENSING_REGISTER.md`).

## Scope exclusions (architected, not built)

Paediatric, neonatal and general-ward content: `nursing_domains` has a `specialty` column and `audience` tags so these can be added without migration pain.
