## Remaining work and governance

- No antibiotic indication review beyond this product's label; the AUC-monitoring consensus is mainly supported by data in **serious invasive MRSA infection**, especially complex bacteremia; avoid universalizing to other organisms, infections or unvalidated clinical settings.
- No dosing, AUC concentration targets, loading doses, infusion rates or treatment selection given. Clinical pharmacy must establish local validated sampling times, PK software/workflow and patient-specific monitoring (e.g., CRRT, obesity, rapidly changing renal function).
- Independent nurse educator, infectious-disease/ICU physician and clinical pharmacist review required; regulatory comparison outside the United States (SFDA, MHRA, EMA) not done.
