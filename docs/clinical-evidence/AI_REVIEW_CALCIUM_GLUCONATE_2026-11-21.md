## Clinical findings and corrections

| Previously incomplete | Specific label-supported issue | Corrected nursing education |
|---|---|---|
| Ceftriaxone warning did not sufficiently separate **neonatal contraindication** from later-age sequential use | The label contraindicates the product in neonates **28 days or younger** receiving ceftriaxone. In all ages, simultaneous administration via shared access risks calcium–ceftriaxone precipitation. For older patients, sequential use is conditional on controlled line management and clinician/pharmacy direction | Add age-specific screening and never treat older sequential use as automatic permission to co-administer simultaneously |
| Rapid-administration cardiovascular harm missing | Hypotension, bradycardia, syncope, arrhythmia and cardiac arrest reported | Expand safety and adverse-effect entries and escalation cues; no rate numbers |
| Hypercalcemia contraindication omitted | Label contraindicates existing hypercalcemia and urges calcium monitoring in renal impairment and with calcium-raising medications | Add ordered calcium and medicine-check cues rather than prescribing lab intervals |
| Insufficient access/tissue caution | Calcinosis cutis and tissue necrosis may occur **with or without** obvious extravasation | Expand site-symptom recognition and clinician-directed local response |
| More precipitating combinations not mentioned | Bicarbonate- or phosphate-containing mixtures can precipitate; calcium with digoxin can aggravate arrhythmia | Warn of medication/access combinations and require pharmacy review without giving compatibility instructions |
| Adverse events underdeveloped | Label reports cardiovascular instability, end-organ injury in neonatal ceftriaxone cases and local severe tissue injury | Replace one-line adverse-effect summary with four organ-specific entries |

## Scope and governance limits

- This label is for a specific **ready-to-use calcium gluconate in sodium chloride** product for acute symptomatic hypocalcemia; it is **not** a calcium chloride product or a general ICU hyperkalemia treatment guideline.
- Clinical dose, serum targets, IV line management and pharmacy preparation remain local and deliberately excluded.
- International SFDA/EMA/MHRA formulations, neonatal and renal-replacement pathways, and evidence for non-label indications need separate full review.
- An independent ICU clinician, pharmacist, and nurse educator must validate bedside use before approving or publishing any clinical material.
- No publication, deployed teaching content or removal of draft warnings.
