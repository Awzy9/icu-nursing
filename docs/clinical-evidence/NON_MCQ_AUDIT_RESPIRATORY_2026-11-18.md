# ICU Nursing non-MCQ clinical audit

18 November 2026. This pass reviewed the bedside assessment, warnings, procedure guidance and examples in the adult oxygen therapy and artificial-airway suctioning lessons. The revision is based on the existing clinical-audit branch at 1207c66. The earlier branch contains separate work on scenarios, medications and the ventilator simulator. Those changes are not included in this pass’s coverage count.

| Location | Finding | Correction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D01-T03 assessment and references | Pulse-oximeter limitations were attributed to NICE delirium guidance. | Use the Saudi Food and Drug Authority’s pulse-oximeter safety communication. Explain that readings may overestimate oxygenation and should be interpreted alongside the patient’s condition. |
| D01-T03 escalation and case | The COPD case said the nurse reported rising drowsiness without describing the immediate respiratory assessment or urgency. | Prioritize airway and breathing assessment, urgent review, oxygen titration within the prescribed protocol and assessment of ventilation and blood gases. |
| D01-T11 responsibilities and procedure table | A signed pressure limit could be read as an instruction to use a stronger vacuum. | Express the adult AARC ceiling as a vacuum magnitude and explain the signed gauge convention. Reinforce the lowest effective suction and the local device and procedure limits. |
| D01-T11 responsibilities | The text deferred entirely to local policy for complications. | State the immediate response to significant deterioration during suctioning and direct persistent or severe deterioration to the airway emergency pathway. |

Sources checked include the [SFDA pulse oximeter safety communication](https://www.sfda.gov.sa/sites/default/files/2026-02/%28SG-2602-434-H%29.pdf), [BTS adult oxygen guideline](https://thorax.bmj.com/content/72/Suppl_1/ii1) and [AARC artificial airway suctioning guideline](https://www.aarc.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/cpg-artificial-airway-suctioning.pdf). The AARC publication’s inconsistent catheter-sizing figures remain explicitly marked in the lesson.
