# ICU Nursing MCQ answer audit

18 November 2026. Covers the 306 authored question-bank MCQs across all 51 sets, plus 39 standalone lab questions and scored cases. The 240 questions shown in both lessons and the bank are counted once. This includes an answer and rationale screen, targeted checks against clinical sources for consequential or ambiguous claims, an independent comparison of every authored answer with the generated website output, and verification of every keyed distractor explanation. The review used practice recommendations, published excerpts, existing evidence notes and current source checks as appropriate. It is an educational content audit; a line-by-line primary-source citation for every distractor and independent clinician sign-off are outside the verified coverage.

## Source and applicability notes

- [AARC suction guideline](https://www.aarc.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/cpg-artificial-airway-suctioning.pdf) gives different catheter-sizing figures in the abstract and table. D01-T11 Q5 retains the discrepancy and directs learners to local policy.
- [UKKA July 2026 guidance](https://guidelines.ukkidney.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/FINAL-VERSION-UKKA-CLINICAL-PRACTICE-GUIDELINE-MANAGEMENT-OF-HYPERKALAEMIA-IN-ADULTS-UPDATED-JULY-2026.pdf) confirms the urgent inpatient 12-lead threshold in its full PDF. The web-summary discrepancy is documented in D04-T06 and the prior batch report.
- [KDIGO 2026 AKI/AKD update](https://kdigo.org/kdigo-2026-aki-akd-guideline-draft-available-for-public-review/) was located as a public-review draft. Questions explicitly keyed to KDIGO 2012 remain edition-specific.
- [AARC oxygen guidance](https://www.aarc.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/cpg-clinical-mangement-adult-o2-acute-settings.pdf), [SSC 2026](https://www.sccm.org/clinical-resources/guidelines/guidelines/surviving-sepsis-campaign-international-guidelines-for-management-of-sepsis-and-septic-shock-2026), [FDA heparin labelling](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/019953s050lbl.pdf), and [NICE NG148](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng148) support the related corrections.
- Lab corrections were checked against the [GCS assessment aid](https://www.glasgowcomascale.org/downloads/GCS-Assessment-Aid-English.pdf), [Alberta CRRT pathway](https://www.albertahealthservices.ca/assets/about/scn/ahs-scn-cc-dialyzingwisely-crrt-alarm-management-care-pathway-resource.pdf), [Lifeblood’s transfusion reaction guidance](https://www.lifeblood.com.au/health-professionals/clinical-practice/adverse-events/FNHTR) and [AARC patient–ventilator assessment](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11298231/).
- Drug questions cite specific US products and editions. Formulation, nursing authority and local protocols may differ. Some source sites return excerpts, generic landings or access errors. Where exact original wording was unavailable, the audit used retrievable alternatives or existing evidence notes. Access limits include the full BSH 2023 transfusion guideline and the primary mechanical-ventilation review indexed as PMC13427949.
