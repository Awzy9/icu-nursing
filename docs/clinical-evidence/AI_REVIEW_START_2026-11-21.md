## Governance and UI wording
The app's expanded warning incorrectly says **“all content is synthetic”**: the app uses real draft nursing lessons, public-source references, and fictional scenarios, not entirely synthetic content. This review updates only the notice **wording** to distinguish unapproved educational drafts from fictional examples. It does **not remove the review disclaimer** or imply any content was approved.

To retire the global draft banner responsibly:
- Establish an explicit inventory of **every learner-visible unit** (40 topics and questions, 50 medications, 25 skills, 20 cases, equipment, practice, calculators, simulations, English/Arabic copy); compare content actually served by the website API to its authored draft.
- For each important factual or numerical claim: source ID/version, exact applicable guideline statement, date/reuse rights, strength/certainty, jurisdiction/population, and a disposition (`verified` / `corrected` / `unsupported` / `requires-local-protocol`).
- Clinically review **every MCQ key, distractor, numerical answer, explanation, scenario branch, simulator calculation and equipment operating statement**, with independent domain-specific human sign-off recorded through the existing authenticated workflow.
- **Clinical reviewer + specialty reviewer; pharmacist for medication content; nurse educator and equipment specialist for procedures.** Maintain separate roles and MFA; no AI may invent or assign reviewer identities. Re-review after material edits and on evidence updates.
- Only after the learner-visible corpus is verified, the relevant reviewer approvals recorded, and release smoke tests pass may a global blanket draft status be retired; keep concise enduring educational-use/local-policy messaging and **per-item** status for remaining drafts.
