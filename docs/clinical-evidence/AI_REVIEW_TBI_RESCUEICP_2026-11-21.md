## Residual clinical and licensing gates

- This is an educational review of selected recommendations, not a nursing-specific protocol or validated alarm/escalation threshold.
- Named neurocritical-care clinician, neurosurgeon and ICU nurse educator review is still required, including EVD and ICP monitor levelling, waveform interpretation, pressure transducer location, reassessment of unusual values, and role/procedure boundaries.
- Do not apply severe-TBI targets indiscriminately to stroke, aneurysmal haemorrhage, infection, paediatric or penetrating brain injury.
- Source access/licence varies: the 2020 update is CC BY 4.0; the 2017 index and 2026 penetrating work remain citation-only unless commercial reuse rights are verified.
- No claims of completion for all 40 topics or the full 166-record clinical corpus; retain the draft banner and clinical governance publication gate.
