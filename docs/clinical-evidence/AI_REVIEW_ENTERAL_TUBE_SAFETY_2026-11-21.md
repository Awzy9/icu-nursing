## Source-by-claim verification

| Claim / issue | Evidence checked | Correction | Remaining gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| External nasogastric tube markers | NHS Healthcare Safety Investigation Branch NG tube placement report: unchanged external length cannot exclude **internal displacement**; markers are only an adjunct after initial position verification | Corrected the D07-T06 module and SK-10/SK-11 assessment, safety and escalation language. Neither unchanged tube markings nor auscultation is stand-alone initial/continued placement proof | Local adult ICU placement verification competency, tube type and manufacturer procedures |
| Initial versus repeat tube checks | NICE CG32 recommendation 1.7.17 and its monitoring table: pH/aspirate pathway with X-ray when needed; external markers may be noted **after initial confirmation**. The NICE table and recommendation can be misread as allowing external markers alone to establish placement | Clearly separate initial confirmation, interval monitoring of external length, and mandatory reassessment after possible displacement, respiratory symptoms or inconclusive checks; withhold feed, water, flushes, medicines until position verified | Local institution pathway, qualified X-ray interpreter, high-risk ICU patient policy |
| pH testing and national differences | Baving et al. 2026 systematic review of six adult-placement guidelines; different approaches to imaging and different pH cutoffs. NICE UK uses pH ≤5.5; other documents may use stricter thresholds | Labeled UK threshold explicitly, avoid inventing a universal internationally valid number | Human specialist verification with the local national standard; acid suppression and aspirate reliability |
| Unverified tube and flushing | NHS England enduring nasogastric safety standard and HSSIB report: do not introduce fluid through an unverified tube; auscultation is not a reliable location test | Emphasized no feeding, medication, water or flush until safe placement is confirmed; escalate rather than interpreting unchanged markings as reassurance | Hospital safety governance, training and product-specific protocol |
