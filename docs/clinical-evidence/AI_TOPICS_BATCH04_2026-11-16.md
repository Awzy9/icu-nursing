## Source-specific findings that matter

1. **Potassium:** UKKA detailed recommendations 14.1/14.2 separate an urgent 12-lead ECG threshold **>=6.0 mmol/L** from continuous monitoring at **>=6.5 mmol/L** or other concerning circumstances. The draft's warning about an inconsistent summary must not confuse these separate recommendations. https://guidelines.ukkidney.org/hyperkalaemia/11-1-ii-hyperkalaemia-in-hospital/11-1-2-ii-hyperkalaemia-in-hospital-14-1-14-2/
2. **TBI:** the official BTF severe TBI fourth-edition guideline continues to provide ICP/CPP thresholds for its defined population. Its guidance site still presents historic RESCUEicp caveats; do not claim newer trial evidence was systematically assessed in this review. https://braintrauma.org/coma/guidelines/severe-tbi
3. **Ventilation modes:** the JICS 2026 review is a current educational overview; it explicitly describes a physiologic approach and limits of proprietary mode nomenclature. Its publication does not convert educational explanations into graded professional guidelines. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13427949/
4. **AKI:** KDIGO's official AKI/AKD page still labels the 2026 guideline **draft under public review**; this status must be refreshed before release. https://kdigo.org/guidelines/acute-kidney-injury/
5. **PADIS:** the official SCCM 2025 focused update supports only limited conditional statements; no blanket antipsychotic recommendation for ICU delirium can be inferred. https://www.sccm.org/clinical-resources/guidelines/guidelines/focused-update-padis-guideline
