## Cross-topic clinical consistency issue

The **2025 ESICM shock guideline** is a cross-reference update for at least **four** inspected files: D02-T08, D02-T04, D02-T09, D02-T12. Only D02-T08 has been corrected thus far (source ID S-ESICM-SHOCK-2025 added as **partial read**, and its incorrect claim about no newer consensus removed). The other three **remain unchanged** and require focused reconciliation of hemodynamic monitoring, source metadata and their MCQ claims.

This should be treated as one coordinated evidence-maintenance issue, rather than four unrelated fixes. The main new guidance belongs to general shock monitoring and differs from the septic-shock-specific SSC recommendations. The 2025 full guideline and supplementary evidence are **not yet** comprehensively read: the information checked so far is the source abstract, introduction and 2014/2025 comparative Table 1.
