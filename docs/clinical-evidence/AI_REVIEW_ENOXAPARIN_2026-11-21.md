# Targeted enoxaparin label-safety audit (MD-44)

**Date:** 2026-11-21  
**Target:** MD-44, educational enoxaparin medication draft (not imported/published; DRAFT, `clinically_reviewed: false`, `pharmacist_reviewed: false`).

## Findings

| Risk | Direct label finding | Correction | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Overbroad heparin-substitution claim | DailyMed Lovenox section 5.6 says enoxaparin is **not interchangeable unit for unit** with unfractionated heparin or other LMWHs because their potency and dosing differ; this is not a global prohibition on every clinician-directed change of therapy | Replaced vague "must not be exchanged" with "not interchangeable unit-for-unit" and instructed that any switch requires a separately authorized order and drug-specific transition pathway | Never provides equivalence conversions or dose recommendations |
| Renal impairment | May 2026 US Lovenox label sections 2.3 and 8.7: drug exposure increases with declining renal function; **severe impairment, CrCl <30 mL/min**, needs indication-dependent dosing adjustment and enhanced monitoring | Added safety flag to verify renal function, order and pharmacist input; withheld all regimen and dose details | Label refers to US branded product; no local renal dosing protocol validated |
| Bleeding, platelet/HIT and neuraxial safety | US label warns of spinal/epidural hematoma with neuraxial procedures; contraindicates active major bleeding and recent/antibody-positive immune HIT; risks may be underestimated in current short draft | Added safety points and reminded learners to escalate neurologic change, bleeding or suspicious platelet trends, without automatically diagnosing HIT from platelet count alone | Full ASH 2018 HIT algorithm and procedure timing not read for this revision |
| Monitoring | Section 2.6: blood counts including platelets, occult blood and clinical bleeding assessment; in selected renal cases anti-factor Xa **may** be used by treating team, while PT/aPTT are **not adequate** to monitor anticoagulant activity | Added practical monitoring reminders and preserved clinician/pharmacist ownership of interpretation | No anti-Xa targets or mandatory universal monitoring frequency taught |
