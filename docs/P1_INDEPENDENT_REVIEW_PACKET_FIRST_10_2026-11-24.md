# ICU Nursing — First ten independent-review packets (not approvals)
**Prepared:** 24 November 2026  
**Scope:** metadata and current draft text from the merged P1 curriculum in `content/drafts`; ordered by **declared gap count** (not by severity of patient harm).  
**Status:** Draft technical/educational review requests. **No human reviewer has signed anything. Do not publish or treat a pass from automated tests as medical verification.**

## Process for each review packet
1. Assign a **qualified named ICU nurse educator** and the specialty reviewer listed below. A trained pharmacist reviews each medicine or medication-dependent scenario.
2. Read the **entire versioned primary source**, including relevant errata and supplemental tables, or record why it cannot be accessed. Compare each scenario *correct answer, incorrect distractor, feedback, monitoring and escalation* with the cited text and current unit policy; document counterexamples.
3. Check jurisdiction-specific scope, protocols, timing, equipment manufacturer differences, product formulation, accessibility, localization and supporting source dates. **Do not introduce prescriptions** into general nursing teaching.
4. Independently confirm **commercial reuse/third-party image rights**, source owner terms, attribution and traceable written permission where needed. A citation and paraphrase do **not automatically confer redistribution rights**.
5. Record any issue in the staff draft register; update only the affected draft through a traceable PR. Re-run validations. A qualified reviewer then documents an actual human approval for the **exact source hash** and derived entry, with independent publisher sign-off.

## Per-entry review checklist

### 1. `SC-11` — Severe hyperkalaemia (8 declared gaps)
- **Specialty:** ICU nursing, nephrology, clinical pharmacy; assess emergency scenario decision scoring.
- **Evidence currently registered:** July 2026 UKKA hospital guideline (`S-UKKA-HK-2026`, RED/citation-only, targeted sections), insulin product information, teamwork reference.
- **Unresolved source conflict:** the draft records different ECG thresholds in UKKA *detailed* guideline 14.1 and its hospital *summary*. Confirm current authoritative wording with a renal clinician/source publisher. Distinguish urgent **12-lead ECG** from continuous monitoring without combining them into an invented universal rule.
- **Reviewer to check:** immediate escalation versus repeat specimen in parallel, ECG observation limits, product-specific calcium/insulin pathways, glucose surveillance and rebound monitoring; scoring of all three case steps.
- **Release blocker:** RED rights; incomplete primary source and local care-pathway review; no human clinical approval.

### 2. `MD-20` — Calcium gluconate (7 gaps)
- **Specialty:** ICU nurse educator, pharmacist, intensivist.
- **Evidence:** `L-CALCIUM` (one US ready-to-use product label) and general FDA/AHRQ/NEWS2 references; indexed YELLOW.
- **Reviewer to check:** the **product label's hypocalcaemia indication** must not be presented as authorization for emergency hyperkalaemia management; avoid transferring product-specific formulations or compatibility instructions to other calcium products. Examine infusion-site injury, cardiac and interaction precautions against local protocol.
- **Release blocker:** no full international/SFDA label comparison, no clinician validation of emergency context or product-rights assessment.

### 3. `SC-09` — Postoperative bleeding (7 gaps)
- **Specialty:** surgical ICU nurse educator, surgeon/intensivist, transfusion specialist, pharmacist.
- **Evidence:** Open RN sections, antithrombotic labels, `S-ISTH-DIC-2025` abstract-level source, ARUP, plus an **OpenStax non-commercial** educational cross-check (RED).
- **Reviewer to check:** urgent patient-first shock evaluation, communication, emergency blood identification, anticoagulant due-dose escalation, and DIC versus other causes of laboratory abnormalities. Do not infer major haemorrhage treatments from the educational cross-check.
- **Release blocker:** local haemorrhage protocol and full source rights, including non-commercial restrictions, missing; answer scores not clinically validated.

### 4. `MD-18` — Magnesium sulfate (6 gaps)
- **Specialty:** ICU pharmacist, nephrology, nurse educator.
- **Evidence:** one US Hospira product label and general infusion/NEWS2 sources (YELLOW).
- **Reviewer to check:** toxic effects and escalation involving respiratory effort, reflexes, circulation, renal impairment, drug interactions, and the difference between concentrated and ready-to-use presentations. Specialist obstetric/arrhythmia protocols are outside this draft's validated scope.
- **Release blocker:** manufacturer/jurisdiction variability and specialist renal/critical care evidence not independently reviewed.

### 5. `SC-01` — Septic shock (6 gaps)
- **Specialty:** ICU nurse educator, intensivist, pharmacist/vascular access team.
- **Evidence:** `S-SSC-2026` at **public recommendation-level, partial** access and RED/citation-only rights; FDA norepinephrine label.
- **Reviewer to check:** early recognition/resuscitation handoff, peripheral-vasopressor access policy when central access would delay support, recognition of extravasation, and continuity of perfusion using only ordered treatment. Check source strength and local escalation policies rather than extrapolate to independent infusion decisions.
- **Release blocker:** incomplete guideline evidence tables, institutional access policy and no clinical approval.

### 6. `SC-05` — Sudden oxygen desaturation (6 gaps)
- **Specialty:** ICU nurse educator, respiratory therapist and intensivist.
- **Evidence:** AARC oxygen/suction guidance (RED), Open RN (GREEN; some undated verification) and generic clinical communication references.
- **Reviewer to check:** emergency airway and patient-first priorities, ventilator/oxygen delivery fault versus dislodgement/obstruction, possible pleural and aspiration emergencies, and whether case options can be misread as independent device operation.
- **Release blocker:** source rights and incomplete equipment-specific / clinical emergency confirmation.

### 7. `SC-14` — Transfusion reaction (6 gaps)
- **Specialty:** transfusion nurse educator, blood bank/transfusion medicine, intensivist.
- **Evidence:** AABB 2024 circular (RED, partial); BSH acute transfusion reaction guideline (**not read**, RED); ISBT education (partial, undated source verification), NICE NG24.
- **Reviewer to check:** immediate stop, identity verification, IV access without infusing implicated residual blood, appropriate blood-bank investigation, severity differentiation, and who may authorize restarting. Align with local blood-bank response procedure.
- **Release blocker:** required full primary source and commercial permission absent; no independent step scoring.

### 8. `SC-16` — Acute hypotension (6 gaps)
- **Specialty:** ICU nursing, intensivist, haemodynamic monitoring specialist.
- **Evidence:** 2025 ESICM shock guideline **summary/abstract only** (RED); older 2014 source, general monitoring and vasoactive product safety.
- **Reviewer to check:** altered consciousness plus low pressure urgent escalation, arterial-line versus cuff discrepancy, false measurement detection *in parallel*, and preservation of perfusion without nurse-independent bolus/vasopressor decisions.
- **Release blocker:** full 2025 recommendations and local haemodynamic/shock protocol review not completed.

### 9. `SC-17` — Delirium and agitation (6 gaps)
- **Specialty:** ICU nurse educator, critical-care pharmacist, delirium/psychiatric liaison specialist.
- **Evidence:** NICE CG103 (partial, undated verification), SCCM 2018/2025 PADIS web recommendations (partial, RED), product label.
- **Reviewer to check:** avoid labelling acute physiological/neurological deterioration as delirium, distinguish arousal eligibility from screening result, avoid reproducing restricted screening instruments, validate non-drug care and antipsychotic uncertainty.
- **Release blocker:** full ICU-specific evidence and tool permissions, local protocols, qualified scoring review.

### 10. `SK-13` — Chest drain monitoring (5 gaps)
- **Specialty:** ICU nurse educator, thoracic/pleural clinician, respiratory therapist.
- **Evidence:** BTS clinical statement (RED/no commercial reuse); targeted Open RN HTML (GREEN with third-party exceptions). Open RN PDF was **not accessed**.
- **Reviewer to check:** adult pleural versus traumatic/postoperative drains, air-leak and water-seal interpretation, digital-system and suction variants, clamping differences, pleural emergency escalation. **Do not** turn a generic monitoring checklist into a universal insertion, removal or troubleshooting procedure.
- **Release blocker:** source reuse restrictions and incomplete device-specific evidence, clinical sign-off pending.

## Handover record — human completion only
For EACH key above, the staff reviewer should record:

| Field | Required before release |
|---|---|
| Qualified primary reviewer | Name, professional role/credentials and dated attestation |
| Specialty reviewer | Name, scope and date (pharmacy/RT/renal/transfusion as applicable) |
| Full-source evidence | Exact editions/URLs or DOI, version and access date; discrepancies and limitations |
| Safety and answer review | Each correct/incorrect option, feedback, escalation, technical scope, and any clinical ambiguity |
| Jurisdiction and unit protocol | Applicability, required localization and unresolved pathways |
| Source/image rights | Permission or applicable commercial reuse rights with proof and expiry where relevant |
| Draft revision | Exact source-draft index hash after review amendments |
| Generated module revision | SHA-256 for the exact released working entry, checked against approval register |
| Publisher | Independent approver, dated release decision and audit trail |
| Outcome | **BLOCKED** until all of the above are completed by real people |
