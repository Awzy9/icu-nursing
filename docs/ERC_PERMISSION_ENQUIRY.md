## Text to send (complete the three bracketed items)
**Subject:** Request for a commercial-use agreement – ERC Guidelines 2025 – ICU nursing education website and app

Dear ERC team,

I am writing on behalf of [organisation / project name – owner to complete], which is developing an independent educational website and mobile app for adult intensive-care nurses ("ICU Nurse Knowledge Map"). The platform teaches assessment, monitoring, escalation, documentation and professional scope. All learning text is originally written. Clinical content is published only after review by appointed, credentialed clinicians; nothing is published yet.

Your Download Centre states that ERC assets may be used for educational purposes free of cost and that commercial use needs an agreement with the ERC. We would like to understand whether and how an agreement applies to our planned use.

1. Planned use. A website and mobile app that may in future be offered under a subscription or institutional licence. The commercial model has not been decided. Free and paid learners would see the same ERC-related content.
2. What we have used so far. We read the ERC Guidelines 2025 Adult Basic Life Support, Adult Advanced Life Support and Post-Resuscitation Care (with ESICM) PDFs and wrote original, unpublished drafts for nurses: (a) cardiac arrest recognition, response and immediate post-ROSC nursing care; (b) defibrillation safety; (c) oxygen safety near defibrillation, and the prevention of in-hospital arrest through early-warning systems. The drafts paraphrase recommendations, cite the guideline (authors, journal, DOI/URL, access date) and do not reproduce ERC figures, algorithms, tables or substantial text.
3. Material we might want to reproduce, if permitted: (i) short quotations of single recommendations with attribution; (ii) simplified, original visuals illustrating steps (not copies of ERC figures); (iii) links to ERC resources; (iv) the ERC algorithms or images themselves – we do not plan this unless you grant permission.
4. Questions. (a) What type of agreement covers commercial or subscription use of paraphrased content, and is an agreement required for paraphrase with citation? (b) Are there fees, royalties, attribution wording or logo-use rules? (c) May we adapt the content for translation (Arabic localisation is planned) and for local protocols? (d) May we write our own quiz questions and scenarios based on the guidelines? (e) What obligations apply when ERC updates the guidelines? (f) Is a separate agreement needed with the publisher (Elsevier) or with ESICM for the joint post-resuscitation guideline? (g) Who is the correct ERC contact for licensing?
5. Safeguards. Original wording; no ERC algorithms, images or tables without written permission; clear "draft / not clinically reviewed" labelling until review; statement that the app does not replace local protocols or current ERC guidance.

Thank you for your guidance.

Kind regards,
[Name, role, organisation, e-mail, telephone – owner to complete]
