# ERC permission enquiry — DRAFT, NOT SENT

Status: prepared for the product owner's approval. **Not sent. No terms agreed. No costs incurred.** Do not send without the owner's explicit approval.

**To:** European Resuscitation Council (contact route to be confirmed by the owner: ERC Download Centre / office; address not verified in this repository)
**Subject:** Request for a commercial-use agreement – ERC Guidelines 2025 – nursing education platform

Dear ERC team,

We are developing an independent educational website and mobile app for adult intensive-care nurses ("ICU Nurse Knowledge Map"). The platform teaches assessment, monitoring, escalation, documentation and scope of practice. All learning text is originally written; clinical content is published only after review by appointed, credentialed clinicians.

Your Download Centre states that ERC assets may be used for educational purposes free of cost and that commercial use needs an agreement with the ERC. We are therefore asking to understand the agreement that would apply.

**1. Planned use.** A website and mobile app that may in future be offered under a paid subscription or institutional licence (the commercial model is not yet decided). Free and paid learners would see the same ERC-related topics.

**2. What we have used so far.** We have read the ERC Guidelines 2025 Adult Basic Life Support, Adult Advanced Life Support and Post-Resuscitation Care PDFs and written two original topic drafts (cardiac arrest recognition and response with immediate post-ROSC nursing care; defibrillation safety). They paraphrase recommendations, always cite the guideline (authors, journal, DOI/URL, access date) and do not reproduce ERC figures, algorithms, tables or substantial text. The drafts are not published.

**3. What we might want to reproduce (please tell us what is permitted, on what terms):**
- short quotations of individual recommendations (for example the numerical compression depth/rate or oxygen targets) with attribution;
- redrawn, simplified, original visuals that illustrate ERC steps (not copies of ERC figures);
- links to ERC resources and the ERC App;
- possible display of the ERC algorithms or images themselves (we do **not** plan this unless you grant permission).

**4. Questions.**
a) What kind of agreement covers commercial or subscription use of paraphrased content, and does paraphrase with citation require an agreement?
b) Are there fees, royalties, attribution wording or logo-use rules?
c) May we adapt content for translation (Arabic localisation is planned) and for different jurisdictions?
d) May we use ERC material in quiz questions and clinical scenarios that we write ourselves?
e) What notice and update obligations apply when ERC revises guidelines?
f) Is a separate agreement needed with the publisher (Elsevier) or with ESICM for the joint post-resuscitation guideline?

**5. Safeguards we will keep:** original wording; no ERC algorithms, images or tables without written permission; clear "draft/not clinically reviewed" labelling until review; a statement that the app does not replace local protocols or current ERC guidance.

We would be grateful for guidance and the contact point for such agreements.

Kind regards,
