## 3. Priority gaps to fix first (clinically consequential, in suggested order)
1. **Safety-critical bedside technique with no source** – arterial-line levelling and maintenance (D02-T04); enteral tube placement confirmation (D07-T06); vasopressor extravasation recognition and management and peripheral-use conditions (D02-T12, D05-T03); first actions after a transfusion reaction (D08-T03). Fix by locating authoritative national patient-safety alerts and professional-body procedures, or leave out of any release.
2. **Potassium and electrolytes** – hypokalaemia, potassium replacement and concentrated-potassium handling (D04-T06; D05-T16 lists concentrated potassium only as an ISMP category).
3. **Extubation and post-extubation care** (D01-T17) – needs ATS/ACCP 2017 liberation guideline and the JICS series part 3 (both not read).
4. **Scale definitions and permissions** – RASS, SAS, CPOT, BPS (D03-T09, D07-T03), plus CAM-ICU/ICDSC (D03-T10): obtain licences or clinician-authored explanations; do not copy charts.
5. **Device-specific content** – CRRT machine, circuit, anticoagulation and alarms (D04-T09); ventilator alarms beyond one HAMILTON-C1 manual (D01-T09); defibrillator and monitor manuals (D02-T15, D02-T02).
6. **Source currency** – SSC 2026 and PADIS full publications (D06-T01, D02-T08, D02-T09, D02-T12, D03-T09, D03-T10, D07-T03); KDIGO 2012 full text and 2026 update; BTS 2017 oxygen; BTF update after RESCUEicp; CDC 2007 isolation; WHO 2009 hand hygiene; AHA 2025.
7. **Neurocritical care beyond TBI** – ICP/CPP for stroke, haemorrhage, hydrocephalus (D03-T03).
8. **ICU-specific assessment** (D07-T01) and a nursing-scope source for each target country (D09-T03).
9. **Coagulation** – national haematology guidance, test physiology and ICU coagulopathy practice (D08-T02).
10. **Pharmacology references** for sedatives and vasopressors (D05-T01, D05-T03).
