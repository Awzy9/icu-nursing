# Nursing documentation
> DRAFT – NOT clinically reviewed. US-oriented source; reviewers must check legal and regulatory norms by jurisdiction.

## 1. Overview
The record is a legal and clinical communication tool. It supports continuity across shifts, quality review and, sometimes, legal proceedings.

## 2. Learning objectives
1. List principles of good documentation.
2. Explain correcting an error properly.
3. Explain why advance charting is falsification.

## 7. Nursing responsibilities
Be objective, factual and professional; chart only what you assessed or did; include date, time and signature; avoid unapproved abbreviations; document promptly after the task; never record something as done when it wasn't; correct errors transparently (paper: single line and "mistaken entry"; electronic: note the correction per system).

## 11. Nursing interventions within scope
| Action | Label |
|---|---|
| Accurate, timely entries | Independent nursing |
| Late entries/corrections per policy | Independent nursing (per policy) |
| Orders and medical diagnoses | Prescriber decision |

## 12. Escalation
Tell the responsible clinician about significant findings first; documentation never replaces a call. Use SBAR (see D09-T01) and record that the call happened.

## 14. Documentation (content)
Assessments, interventions, response, communication/escalation, education.

## 17. MCQs
**Q1.** You gave a medicine but got interrupted before charting. Best practice?
A. Chart later as if on time. B. Document promptly with the actual time. C. Skip. D. Ask a colleague to chart.
**Answer: B.**
**Q2.** Charting a task before doing it is:
A. Efficient. B. Falsification. C. Required. D. Optional.
**Answer: B.**
**Q3.** Paper error correction:
A. White-out. B. Single line, "mistaken entry", initials. C. Scribble. D. Tear page.
**Answer: B.**
**Q4.** Which is objective?
A. "Patient seems upset." B. "Patient crying, RR 24." C. "Bad night." D. "Difficult."
**Answer: B.**
**Q5.** A colleague observed a finding. You should:
A. Chart it as yours. B. Document only what you personally assessed and attribute others. C. Delete. D. Ignore.
**Answer: B.**
