## Additional gaps found
- **Jurisdiction:** most sources are US or UK; no regulator document for scope of practice outside the US (D09-T03) and no non-US pathways.
- **Monitoring standards:** AHA ECG monitoring standards and Joint Commission alarm resources were inaccessible (403); ICU monitor alarm limits are untaught.
- **Neurocritical care:** BTF applies to severe adult TBI only; no non-TBI ICP guidance (D03-T03).
- **ICU-specific assessment:** D07-T01 rests on a general entry-level checklist.
- **Coagulation:** D08-T02 has no national guideline; physiological explanations and ICU coagulopathy practice unsupported.
- **Paediatrics and neonates:** not covered anywhere; adult only.

## Prioritisation principle
Fix first what could cause patient harm if taught wrongly or omitted (technique and medication-safety gaps), then currency of superseded sources, then breadth. If adequate evidence cannot be found, keep the topic incomplete or exclude it from any release rather than invent content.
